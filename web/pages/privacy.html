Sum 32 · Privacy

Ronda 32 Games · Last updated October 31, 2026 · Short version, a fuller policy will replace it.

What we keep

  • Your Telegram user ID and first name (plus the initial of your last name), to show you on the daily leaderboard.
  • Your Daily games: moves, score and time, so the server can check the score.
  • Which days you opened the game, how many games you finished and how long the game was open.

We do not keep your phone number, your @username, your contacts or your location. We do not sell data or show ads.

Why

To run the leaderboard, stop cheating, and see how many people play and come back so we can make the game better. Reports use a code per player, never your name.

How long

While the game is running. If you ask us to delete your data, everything linked to your Telegram ID is erased at once.

Delete your data

Message @ronda32games_bot and ask for your data to be deleted.

Who runs the servers

The game is served from GitHub Pages and the server runs on Cloudflare. Telegram's own terms apply inside Telegram.
